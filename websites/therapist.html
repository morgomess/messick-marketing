The Process

What working together
actually looks like.

01
Free Consultation

A 15-minute call to see if we're a good fit. No pressure, no intake forms yet - just a conversation.

02
Your First Session

We go at your pace. You don't have to have it all figured out. The first session is just us getting to know each other.

03
Finding Your Rhythm

Most people start weekly. We check in regularly about what's working and adjust as you go.

04

Therapy isn't linear. You'll have hard weeks. The goal is that over time, the good days start to outnumber the hard ones.

Common Questions

The things people usually
wonder before reaching out.

You don't have to be in crisis to deserve support. Most people wait years - until things have gotten much harder - before reaching out. If something feels off and it's affecting your daily life, that's enough reason. You don't have to earn it.
I'm an out-of-network provider. Many clients receive partial reimbursement through their insurance using a Superbill I provide. I also offer a sliding scale - cost should not be the reason you don't get support. Let's talk about it on the consultation call.
Fit matters enormously. If therapy didn't work, it's often because the approach or the relationship wasn't right - not because therapy itself doesn't work for you. I'd rather you find the right person (even if it's not me) than give up on the process entirely.
Yes - I offer both in-person sessions at my Denver office and telehealth to anyone in Colorado. Many clients find telehealth just as effective, and it removes a significant barrier to showing up consistently.
That depends entirely on what you're working on and what you want from therapy. Some people come for 12 weeks with a specific goal. Others work with me for a year or more and find the ongoing support valuable. We revisit this regularly - you're never stuck.
