Search

Pick a client and what you're hunting for. The app builds the searches, then either runs them itself or hands you a pack to run in your logged-in browser.

1 · Who and what

Keywords are seeded from the client's expertise areas. Edit freely — one per line.
Each line becomes its own search on every platform selected below.

2 · Minimum bar

Nothing below the bar is thrown away — it is scored, labelled, and parked in Below bar on the Queue so you can still see it.

3 · Run it

YouTube can be searched directly. X, LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook have no usable search API, so those run through your own logged-in browser.

Queue

Ranked by combined score. Thumbs up or down teaches the scorer what you actually want — that feedback drives the weekly recalibration on the Calibrate tab.

Comments

Every approved post gets a drafted comment in that client's voice. Nothing posts from here. You approve each draft, then the drop pack carries them to your browser.

Drafts only. This app never posts. It produces text you approve and a pack you run yourself, which keeps a human between the model and a live clinical account.

Calibrate

Your thumbs up/down votes are compared component by component. Where a component reliably separates the posts you liked from the ones you didn't, it earns weight. Where it doesn't discriminate, it loses weight.
