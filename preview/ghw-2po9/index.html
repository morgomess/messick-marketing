Good to know

Questions, answered.

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged in full, which is why a card on file is needed to book.

Payment

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, debit and cash. Affirm lets you pay over time on any service.

Is this a good place for my first massage?

Yes. Victor walks you through everything before you start, and you can ask him to change the pressure or skip an area at any point.

Deep Tissue or Swedish?

Deep Tissue for stubborn tightness and recovery after hard training or long shifts. Swedish if you mostly want to unwind. Not sure? Book either and tell Victor, and he'll adjust.

What should I wear?

Whatever's comfortable. For table massage you undress to your comfort level and stay covered by a sheet the whole time. Chair massage is fully clothed.

Can I get a massage while pregnant?

Yes. Request a Prenatal Massage on Vagaro, and Victor will call you for a quick 5-minute consult before your appointment.

How far do mobile sessions go?

Statesboro and the surrounding region. A per-mile travel fee applies beyond Statesboro, measured from the studio.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking right at the studio, and the studio is wheelchair accessible.
