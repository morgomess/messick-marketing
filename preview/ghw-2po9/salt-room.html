Benefits of a salt room

Salt therapy, or halotherapy, means relaxing in a room with fine, dry salt in the air. It grew out of the old salt caves of Europe, and here’s why people try it.

Breathe easy

Many people say breathing feels clearer after a session.

Stress relief

45 still minutes to let the day go.

Better rest

Some people say they sleep better that night.

Recovery days

A calm reset between hard training days.

Refreshed skin

People often say their skin feels soft and refreshed.

Screen-free time

A real break from phones and noise.

These are what people commonly report, not medical claims. If you have a breathing condition, check with your doctor first.
