Questions, answered

Cancellation and payment details are on the booking page.

Is this a good place for my first massage?

Yes. Victor walks you through it, and you can change the pressure any time.

Deep Tissue or Swedish?

Deep Tissue for stubborn tightness. Swedish to unwind. Either way, he adjusts.

What should I wear?

Whatever's comfortable. You're covered by a sheet the whole time.

Can I get a massage while pregnant?

Yes. Request Prenatal on Vagaro and Victor calls you for a 5-minute consult first.

How far do mobile sessions go?

Statesboro and nearby towns. A per-mile fee applies outside Statesboro.

What is a salt room session like?

You sit back, fully clothed, and breathe for 45 minutes. Nothing else to do.

Is there parking?

Yes, free and right out front. Wheelchair accessible.
