About

Who We Are

Pressley Welding Inc. was founded in 2000 by current Owner and President, Jerry Pressley. After over 15 years in the industry, Jerry decided to start his own steel erection company, mainly specializing in miscellaneous steel projects.

Jerry has been aided by his wife and office manager, Stacy Pressley, since beginning operations.

Services

What We Do

Under the management of Jerry Pressley and our experienced crews, we have completed some of the most complex and challenging miscellaneous steel projects in the Carolinas.

However, we have also completed hundreds of typical miscellaneous projects such as installing roof frames, lintels, bracing, and structural members for floor infills.

  • Roof frames
  • Lintels
  • Bracing
  • Floor infills
  • Metal decking
  • Misc. steel
AWS Certified

All of our welders are certified in accordance to the American Welding Society D1.1/D1.1M & D1.3-08 Structural Welding Code, and certified to weld unlimited thicknesses of material in all positions along with galvanized metal decking.
