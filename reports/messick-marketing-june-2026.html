The Story This Period

What the numbers say

Our LinkedIn and Instagram channels are performing at or above industry benchmarks, with Facebook engagement at 47.37% (4x the typical 0.3–0.8% range). The real story: we published 34 posts but earned only 7 likes and 18 comments, meaning our audience is reading and responding to specific themes — notably HIPAA misconceptions and specialty practice positioning — rather than engaging broadly.

The Plan

What we recommend next

1
Consolidate to LinkedIn + Instagram text; pause Facebook and YouTube reels for 30 daysHigh
LinkedIn text posts on HIPAA and specialty practice strategy earned 740 impressions on 3 posts (247/post) vs. Facebook's 1,697 impressions on 9 posts (189/post) but with 7x deeper comment engagement. Text format averaged 206 impressions vs. video's 82. We'll cut production load by 50% and redirect effort toward the two platforms and formats that prove our messaging.
🎯 Increase impressions per post from 87 to 150+ within 30 days; reduce production load by 12 posts monthly.
2
Publish 2–3 LinkedIn text posts weekly on compliance barriers, specialty practice positioning, and case study insightsHigh
Our top 3 posts (440, 171, 128 impressions) all centered on HIPAA myths and practice differentiation on LinkedIn. We posted 12 times in June and the pattern is clear: this audience hungers for regulatory and competitive intelligence. LinkedIn's 1.58% engagement is within benchmark and our impression-per-post efficiency (79/post for LinkedIn) suggests room to increase volume without diminishing returns.
🎯 Drive 50+ comments and 200+ impressions per week from LinkedIn within 60 days.
3
Pause YouTube uploads; reallocate video production toward LinkedIn carousels with case study visualsHigh
YouTube earned 183 views on 6 posts (30.5/post) with 0% engagement and 1 dislike. LinkedIn text posts averaged 247 impressions with higher interaction depth. We'll test a carousel format on LinkedIn that embeds before/after visuals, client testimonials, or process workflows — leveraging the video production effort but matching LinkedIn's proven content appetite.
🎯 Generate 15+ impressions per video carousel on LinkedIn within 45 days; measure comment and click-through lift vs. text-only posts.
4
Repurpose top 3 LinkedIn posts as Instagram carousels or short-form graphicsMedium
The three LinkedIn text posts that earned 440, 171, and 128 impressions contain high-value claims (HIPAA misconceptions, specialty practice differentiation). Instagram's 41.67% engagement rate and 2-follower base means these will reach a micro-audience, but the format shift will test whether the same messaging performs as a carousel or static graphic. If successful, we can apply this to future ideation.
🎯 Reach 50+ Instagram accounts in 30 days and capture 3–5 saves or shares per repurposed post.
5
Audit and fix Instagram follower-growth funnel; test link-in-bio CTA on top-performing postsMedium
Instagram has the highest engagement rate (41.67%) but only 2 followers and a 72% drop in impressions month-over-month. Our top Instagram asset — the Luna Bloom navigator video — reached 105 people but generated 0 likes or comments, suggesting no clear conversion path. We'll add a link-in-bio CTA to the top 3 Instagram posts (the navigator video, the IR rebrand case study image, and the HIPAA myth graphic) and measure clicks to website, LinkedIn, or lead form to close the loop.
🎯 Capture 5+ website clicks or LinkedIn profile visits from Instagram within 30 days; grow Instagram followers to 5+ by end of Q3.
